Fresh Fruit.

Only the fruit you like. None of the fruit you don't.

Gas station fruit cups are soggy and full of fruit you didn't pick. Buying it whole means pounds of fruit to wash, cut, and finish before it goes bad. Ours are made to order with exactly the fruit you want.

Fresh Vibes.

Amber and Elizabeth first got the idea for Dave & Marbie's in 2025. The idea was born from one simple problem: there's nowhere downtown to get a "fun little treat."

Options in Menomonie range from politically charged corporate coffee to niche coffee shops that only appeal to health-conscious people. And sure, there's ice cream downtown, but nowhere to get a more fruit-focused treat.

We wanted something different.
