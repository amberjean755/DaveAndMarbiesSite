Fresh Fruit.

Only the fruit you like. None of the fruit you don't.

Gas station fruit cups are soggy and full of fruit you didn't pick. Buying it whole means pounds of fruit to wash, cut, and finish before it goes bad. Ours are made to order with exactly the fruit you want.
